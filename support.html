Watchtile Support

Getting your watch face onto the Home Screen

Add a Home Screen widget

  1. Open Watchtile and pick a face.
  2. Tap Use This Face.
  3. Go to your Home Screen and press and hold an empty area.
  4. Tap Edit, then Add Widget.
  5. Choose Watchtile, pick a size, and tap Add Widget.

Add a Lock Screen complication

  1. Press and hold your Lock Screen, then tap Customise.
  2. Tap the area below the clock and choose Watchtile.

Changing the face on a widget

Choose a different face in the app and tap Use This Face. Your widgets follow it — you do not need to remove and re-add them.

Restoring purchases

On a new device, open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so there is nothing to buy again.
