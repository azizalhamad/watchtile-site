Privacy Policy

Watchtile · last updated 20 September 2026

Watchtile does not collect any data about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDK that could do any of those things on our behalf.

What stays on your device

The app stores the watch face you have chosen and how you have styled it — colours, markers, numerals, hands, typeface and the text you sign the dial with. This is kept in your device's own storage so that the app and its widgets can read it. It never leaves your device, and we have no way to see it.

What we never do

  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts or location.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not show advertising or share anything with advertisers.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not operate a server that the app talks to.

Purchases

Watchtile offers one-time purchases to unlock collections of faces. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app asks Apple only whether a purchase is owned, so it knows what to unlock; that check goes to Apple, not to us.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
